What is Ajolo?

One app instead of three

Ajolo combines three things that usually live in separate apps: logging workouts, tracking food and building habits. Streaks and ranks show you how you are doing. Your data stays on your device, and you do not need an account.

  1. Get the appFor iPhone and Android. The basic features are free.
  2. Create a profileNo email address needed, right on your device.
  3. Get startedAdd a habit, start a workout or log your first meal.

Features

Three areas, one flow

Routines, gym and nutrition live in the same app. Hide what you do not need in the settings.

Gym

You start an empty workout or a routine, log your sets and see your values from last time. The rest timer runs on its own.

  • Set types: normal, warm-up, drop set, to failure, plus RPE and supersets
  • 1,394 exercises with video, custom exercises possible
  • 10 ready-made plans and a plan based on your answers, calculated on your device
  • Front and back body view with recovery status for 17 muscle groups
  • Heart rate via Bluetooth strap, Apple Health or Health Connect

Nutrition

You log meals, scan barcodes or take a photo of your food. You see calories and macros per day, plus water and steps.

  • Barcode scanner, favorites, your own products and recipes
  • AI photo scan and voice input (Pro, up to 10 each per day): Ajolo estimates nutrition values and you can correct everything
  • 100 recipes sorted by meal
  • Calorie needs based on Mifflin-St Jeor

Routines and streaks

Create habits daily, for specific weekdays or as "x times per week". Each day is done, skipped or missed. Your streak shows how long you stay on track.

Statistics

Calories, water, macros and steps as charts over a week, month or year (with Pro). In the gym area you see consistency, muscle distribution, measurements and progress photos.

Health data

Ajolo reads steps, active calories, distance and heart rate from Apple Health or Health Connect. It writes nothing.

Widgets

Five widgets each on iOS and Android, for example for streak and nutrition.

Progress photos

Nine poses, before-and-after comparison and a time-lapse video.

Backup and transfer

Back up your data as a file and move to a new device over Wi-Fi. The Wi-Fi transfer is not encrypted.

Custom exercises

If an exercise is missing, add it yourself, tracked by weight, time or cardio.

German and English

The app follows your language, with German as fallback.

Ranks

Eight ranks, three ways up

Gym, nutrition and routines each have their own rank ladder. Every level exists three times, from Iron to Master. Ajolo Elite sits at the top.

Gym

Your overall rank grows with every completed workout, and Ajolo Elite starts at 88 workouts. On top come ranks per muscle and per exercise, calculated from strength standards.

Nutrition

Every day earns points: if you land within 200 kcal of your daily goal (including burned calories) you get 2 points, within 400 kcal you get 1. A day with a bigger gap earns nothing, a day without entries costs one point.

Routines

For each habit, a completed day counts plus 1 and a miss minus 1. Your routine rank is the average of all active habits.

Privacy

Your data stays on your device

Ajolo has no server of its own and no user account. Profile, training, nutrition, notes and photos stay on your device. No advertising or analytics software is built in.

When data leaves your device

  • AI photo: a downsized image and your optional note go to the Google Gemini API.
  • AI voice: your device turns speech into text (depending on the device via Apple or Google servers), the text goes to Gemini and parts of it to Open Food Facts.
  • Barcode and food search: a request to Open Food Facts.
  • Connection check: the app briefly checks your internet connection via Google and Cloudflare.
  • Subscription and codes: RevenueCat, with a pseudonymous identifier.

Full details are in the privacy policy.

Questions

Short answers

How much does Ajolo cost?

The app is free. Ajolo Pro costs €39.99 per year or €9.99 per month. You cancel in your Apple or Google account, at the latest 24 hours before the end of the current period.

How accurate is the photo scan?

It gives estimates. You see the result and can adjust ingredients and amounts before you save it.

Do I need an account?

No. At the start you create a profile on your device, without an email address.

What is the minimum age?

16 years. Under 18, parental consent is required.

Does Ajolo replace a doctor or trainer?

No. Ajolo is a tracker and not a medical device. If you have complaints or pre-existing conditions, talk to a doctor first.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 16 or newer, Android devices with Android 8.0 or newer.
